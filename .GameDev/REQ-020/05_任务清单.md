## 变更摘要

### Client.client.lua (+5行)
- 在 `isMobile` 检测后、模块加载前添加:
  ```lua
  if isMobile then
      local playerGui = player:WaitForChild("PlayerGui")
      playerGui.ScreenOrientation = Enum.ScreenOrientation.LandscapeSensor
  end
  ```
- `LandscapeSensor` 支持左右横屏自动切换
- `isMobile` 条件确保只在移动端生效，PC/Studio 不受影响
